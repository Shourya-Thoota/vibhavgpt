Install in three steps

  1. Download the installer, then open it from your browser or your Downloads folder.
  2. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", select More info, then Run anyway.
  3. VibhavGPT installs in a few seconds and opens by itself. Sign in, and find it later in the Start menu or on your desktop.

SmartScreen shows that warning because the installer is not yet code-signed. Only run installers downloaded from this official VibhavGPT address, and check the checksum first.

What's new

  • One-click install: no setup pages, and VibhavGPT opens as soon as it is ready.
  • The window buttons blend into the title bar instead of sitting on a box of their own.
  • A native Windows title bar that is part of the app, with Snap Layouts on Windows 11.
  • Smooth window dragging, and window buttons that follow your light or dark theme.
  • Closing the window keeps VibhavGPT in the system tray, ready to reopen or start a new chat.
  • Press Ctrl + Shift + Space to bring VibhavGPT back from anywhere.
