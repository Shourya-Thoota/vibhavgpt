# Security policy

VibhavGPT is operated by Pansophy LLC. We take security reports seriously and thank you for helping keep the service and its users safe.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **security@pansophy.com**. Please do not open a public issue or pull request for a security problem, and do not discuss it publicly until we have had a chance to fix it.

A useful report includes:

- what the problem is and which part of VibhavGPT it affects (the web app, the API, the Windows app or the Android app);
- the steps to reproduce it, or a short proof of concept;
- what an attacker could do with it;
- the version, commit or date you tested, and your contact details if you want a reply.

We read every report and will reply to the address you write from. We handle reports in confidence and will tell you when the problem is fixed. We may ask you for more detail along the way. If you would like to be credited for the finding, say so in your report.

## Supported versions

Only the latest `main` branch, and the service running from it at vibhavgpt.com, receive security fixes. The Windows and Android apps load the live service, so fixes to the service reach them without an update; for the apps themselves, use the newest release on the official download pages.

## Good-faith research

We will not pursue action against research that is done in good faith and follows these rules:

- Test only against your own account and your own data.
- Do not read, change or delete other people's data. If you come across any, stop and tell us.
- Do not run denial-of-service, spam or brute-force tests, and do not use social engineering or physical attacks against our people or providers.
- Give us reasonable time to fix a problem before you share it.

Findings in software or services we do not run (for example the platforms and providers we depend on) are best reported to their owners.

## Our privacy guarantee

The same promises apply to everything we do with a report and to everything in the product:

- **We never train AI models on your chats.** Conversations are not used to train models.
- **We never sell your data.**
- **We never show ads.**
- **Your data stays private.** Memory is yours to see, edit and delete, and you can delete your account and its data from the app.

Please keep real user data out of your reports. If you must show impact, use your own test account.
